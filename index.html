How

Five moves. Nothing written to your software.

Read

Connectors read your files, your databases and your APIs. Nothing is ever written to your software.

Repair

In an export, dates, accents, amounts and old formats are corrected during ingestion. An unreadable row is set aside with the reason it was rejected.

Merge

Several sources become one record. Their disagreements stay visible.

Watch

Every read is timestamped. A record left unchanged past its declared limit appears in the decision inbox.

Serve

One app per team on the phone, an API, SQL, webhooks. With Pro, questions in plain language.

Nothing is lost without being counted. On every run, rows read equal rows written plus rows set aside.

Trust

What your IT team can verify.

  • Read-only. lumnik never writes to your systems.
  • Isolated by the database. PostgreSQL separates every organization's rows.
  • Self-hosted. The hub runs on your servers. With Pro, the AI model can too.
  • Reversible. Plain PostgreSQL tables, a standard pg_dump.
