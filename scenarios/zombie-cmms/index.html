A replaced CMMS kept running for a few audits a year

Goal Read and archive a replaced CMMS's thirty years of work orders, then switch it off and still answer the audits that cite an order.

A replaced CMMS stays on for a few compliance reads a year. lumnik reads it one last time, archives what it read, and the machine is switched off.

  • 3,600 orders read by the usual schedule
  • one S3 archive under one digest
  • after the switch-off: the same row, the same bytes

Thirty years of work orders, read by the schedule

The JDBC connector keeps its usual schedule; the replay runs it every minute. The account has SELECT only, its password is a hub secret.

  • 3,600 orders from 1995 to 2024
  • the run starts from the schedule, not by hand

Limits

  • A connector whose source is switched off fails at every slot until it is paused; the hub does not pause it by itself.
  • lm source jdbc discover still quotes the driver's own message.
  • Retention on the bucket (object lock) belongs to the store, not the hub.
  • Simulated CMMS and bucket; synthetic data.

Maintenance · compliance

A replaced CMMS is still running.

For a few reads a year.

A licence, a machine, and one person who knows how to restart it.

Simulated CMMS · synthetic data

The problem

Thirty years of work orders keep the machine alive.

  • 3,600 orders, from 1995 to 2024.
  • An audit can cite any of them.
  • Switching off means losing the answer.

Nothing is migrated

The CMMS is read as it is.

  • A read-only account.
  • No data migration.
  • No application to build.

What had to be written

filename: work-orders-final.csv
mode: snapshot

One archive, one name, delivered once.

A 63-line scenario, three declaration files.

The proof

One digest.

  • The local snapshot.
  • The export's receipt.
  • The object read back from the bucket.

SHA-256 6cfa21ba…92fc2, replay of 3 November 2026.

The machine switched off

The next run says why it fails.

A pause stops the schedule.

The limit

The hub does not pause itself.

  • Without a pause, every slot fails.
  • Bucket retention belongs to the storage.
  • discover still shows the driver's message.
