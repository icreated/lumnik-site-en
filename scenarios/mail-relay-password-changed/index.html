The SMTP relay changed its password: three cold-room alerts do not leave, the on-call desk knows

Goal Mail a cold room's temperature excursions, know the same day that a mail did not leave and why, then send it again without a duplicate.

A pharmaceutical wholesaler stores its medicines between 2 and 8 °C in two cold rooms. The data logger exports its readings once a day; an excursion is mailed to the quality lead, through the company's SMTP relay.

  • on Monday, the alert of CF-2 leaves;
  • overnight, IT changes the sending account's password, the hub keeps the old one;
  • on Tuesday, the compressor of CF-1 stops: three mails given up, the on-call desk told by a webhook, the ERROR column says what to fix.

The excursion of CF-2 is mailed

The hub sends through the company's relay, with the alertes-qualite account.

  • the csv-file connector reads the day's export: 48 readings, one per room and per hour
  • the Process raises a decision above 8 °C
  • a rule mails it to the quality lead; a webhook waits for given-up mails
  1. 1
    The subject is the sentence

    The quality lead reads the room, the temperature, the hour and the probe in the inbox, not an event name.

What this step proves

An excursion reaches the quality lead's inbox, its sentence as the subject.

IT changes the sending account's password

The password policy asks for a change every season.

  • the relay accepts only the new one
  • the hub keeps the old one: nobody thought of it
  1. 1
    A service account

    The sending account is nobody's: its password changes without the person who put it in the hub knowing.

What this step proves

The failure comes neither from the hub nor from the data: from a setting changed elsewhere.

The compressor of CF-1 stops: three mails given up, the on-call desk told

Three readings above 8 °C between 2 and 4 a.m.: three decisions, three mails.

  • each mail is tried three times, then given up
  • the ERROR column says authentication refused (SMTP 535): the relay refuses the password
  • the on-call desk's webhook receives one notification.mail.given-up per mail, with its ID
  1. 1
    The gesture is in the message

    SMTP 535: fix LUMNIK_SMTP_PASSWORD. An unreachable relay would say relay unreachable: fix the host or the network.

What this step proves

A mail that does not leave is known the same day, by a road other than mail, with what to fix.

Limits

  • The replayed mail arrives after the outage: here, hours after the excursion. The hub does not block the lots; it tells.
  • A changed password gives up every mail: one event per mail, not one per outage.
  • The webhook carries the mail's ID and the cause, never the alert's text: the on-call desk knows a mail must be sent again, not what it said.
  • Mails are sent again one by one.
  • Passwords change in the hub's configuration, with a restart.
  • Readings, rooms and probes synthetic; the relay is a local Mailpit.

Cold rooms · 2-8 °C

An excursion is mailed.

To the quality lead, through the company's SMTP relay.

temp_c>8

The problem

The relay changes its password. The hub keeps the old one.

  • Three alerts of CF-1 do not leave.
  • The quality lead receives nothing, and does not know there was something to receive.

What is not changed

The relay and the data logger stay as they are.

  • The export is the data logger's own.
  • The hub logs in to the relay like any other software.

What had to be written

lm integration add relay-watch \
  --events notification.mail.given-up

A given-up mail tells the on-call desk.

  • Then the new password.
  • And lm notify replay for each mail.

What was fixed

A refused password and a dead relay read the same (#1905).

  • A given-up mail is an event (#1906).
  • The mail's subject is the decision's sentence (#1910).
