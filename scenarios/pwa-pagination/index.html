Link the views by name

Each View picks its columns. Links filter by name, both ways.

  • the category id is not shown
  1. 1
    Chosen columns

    A link can only name a displayed column.

  2. 2
    Round trip

    Category to its products; product to its category.

What this step proves

You move between views with no technical ID on screen.

manifests/30-products-view.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Viewmetadata:  name: pwa-pagination-productsspec:  scope: pwa-pagination  table: connector.v_pwapaginationproduct  title: Catalogue des articles  columns: [product_id, reference, description, category_name, active]  links:    - column: category_name      targetView: pwa-pagination-categories      filters:        name: "{category_name}"

The problem

Loading everything freezes the screen.

  • 40,000 rows do not fit on a phone.
  • Naive paging skips or repeats rows.
  • Categories sit in another file.

Nothing is changed

Two CSV exports, as they are.

  • An app reduced to records.
  • No application to build.
