One batch receipt date written three ways

Goal Compare a batch's receipt date in the LIMS, the ERP and the carrier's report on the plant's day, to keep only typing mistakes in dispute.

A plant receives its batches of raw material. Three systems say when: the LIMS scans the sample (an instant), the ERP books the receipt (a date), the carrier writes "Livré le" in a CSV (text).

  • read as they are, the carrier's text makes the column text: twelve batches in dispute;
  • typed, a date meets a time at the UTC day: the night shift's batch is in dispute;
  • with zone: Europe/Paris, one dispute is left, the real typing mistake.

Twelve batches, three ways to say the same date

The LIMS and the ERP in one PostgreSQL, the carrier's report as a CSV.

  • lims.sample.received_at: timestamptz, the instant of the lab's scan
  • erp.goods_receipt.receipt_date: date, typed by the warehouse clerk
  • livraisons.csv, ISO-8859-1: "Livré le" as dd/MM/yyyy
  1. 1
    Two batches that matter

    L-0104 arrives with the night shift: scanned at 00:30 on the 6th, Paris time. L-0109: the clerk booked the receipt on the 4th, the batch arrived on the 6th.

What this step proves

Each system keeps its type: an instant, a date, text.

Read as they are: twelve batches in dispute

Two connectors, one entity mapping the three columns to received.

  • received_at lands as timestamptz, receipt_date as date
  • delivered_on stays text: nothing types it
  • one text source makes the fused column text: "05/11/2026" is not "2026-11-05"
  1. 1
    The hub does not guess

    01/07 may be 1 July or 7 January: the hub does not choose for the carrier. The column stays text, and every batch is in dispute.

What this step proves

A date written as text is never read as a date by chance.

A parse-date added later: the apply says so

The carrier's format is declared: parse-date as dd/MM/yyyy.

  • lm apply warns: the table keeps the text type it was created with
  • the run reads the file again: a changed mapping is not "unchanged"
  • the gestures: delete the entity, delete the connector, apply it, run it, then apply the entity
  1. 1
    Typed, one false dispute left

    The column is a timestamptz. A date and a time compare at the day: L-0104 is in dispute, because at 23:30 UTC it is still the 5th. L-0109 is the real disagreement.

What this step proves

The integrator reads what to do when applying, not after a silent run.
