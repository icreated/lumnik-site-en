An invoice with an unreadable amount waits for its fix at the source

Goal Never write an invoice without an amount, and follow each set-aside line until it is fixed.

An export of supplier invoices carries two mistyped amounts and a test invoice.

  • the three set aside, as they were read;
  • the test invoice discarded;
  • the other two fixed at the source, their letter closed by the right read.

The problem

Unreadable amounts.

  • 1.250.00
  • 87.5O, with the letter O.

What is not changed

The ERP stays the reference.

  • The fix is made at the source.
  • Nothing is touched up in the hub.

The trap

An invoice written with no amount.

  • It goes into the totals.
  • Nobody looks for it.

What had to be written
