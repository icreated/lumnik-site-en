An Excel export with merged cells: every line with its region and client.

Sales send a monthly Excel report: one group per client, the region and the client in merged cells, a subtotal under each group. Saved as CSV, a merged cell keeps its value on the group's first row only; the rows below are empty. Sales want every line with its region and client.

Limits
