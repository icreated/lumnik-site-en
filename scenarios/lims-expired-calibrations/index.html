Results measured on an instrument out of calibration

Goal Link every LIMS result to its instrument's calibration certificate, and be told when it was measured after the end of validity, even when the register changes afterwards.

A metrology register and a LIMS, two CSV exports: 6 instruments, 144 results.

  • an Entity joins the register onto every result;
  • a Process compares two columns of the joined row: measured_at after valid_until;
  • the register changes: the results already there are judged again.

The validity on every result

The Entity is keyed by result. Its join fetches the instrument's line from the register.

  • match: result_id
  • joins: from instrument_id, three register columns
  • in October, every result is in calibration
  1. 1
    A lookup, not a fusion

    A join enriches the result rows. It never adds one.

What this step proves

One row now holds the measurement and its instrument's validity.

A rule on the joined row

The Process listens on the Entity's view, connector.v_calib_result_a.

  • "measured_at>{valid_until}": braces name a column, not a text
  • key: result_id, the decision carries the result number
  1. 1
    Applied after October

    A Process does not judge the rows already there: only new rows, and rows that change.

What this step proves

A rule compares two columns that come from two systems.

manifests/30-out-of-calibration.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Processmetadata:  name: scenario-calib-expired-aspec:  scope: qc-lab-a  on: { event: data.row.changed, table: connector.v_calib_result_a }  when:    - "measured_at>{valid_until}"      # measured after the instrument's certificate ran out  emit:    type: decision.out-of-calibration-a    message: "{result_id} ({sample}, {analyte}) measured on {instrument_id} at {measured_at}: certificate {certificate} valid until {valid_until}"    key: result_id

Limits

  • The conditions are ANDed: 'measured before the calibration' needs a second Process.
  • A date alone is midnight UTC: a certificate valid until 2 November ends at the start of the 2nd.
  • A deleted result, or one whose number changes, is not judged.
  • The results already there when the Process is applied are judged only if they or their instrument change.
  • Synthetic instruments, certificates and results.

Laboratory · ISO 17025

A result counts only if its instrument was calibrated.

The register and the results live in two systems.

6 instruments, 144 results, two CSV exports

The problem

BAL-03's certificate ran out on 2 November.

  • It kept weighing on the 3rd, 4th and 5th.
  • The two exports are compared by hand.

What is not changed

The LIMS and the register stay as they are.

  • Their usual CSV exports.
  • No development in either system.

What had to be written

joins: [{ table: register, from: instrument_id }]
when: ["measured_at>{valid_until}"]

One join, one condition between two columns.

  • An Entity: the validity on every result.
  • A Process: one decision per result.

The register changes

HPLC-01 fails its check of 27 October.

  • Its validity is shortened in the register.
  • 8 results already validated come back up.
