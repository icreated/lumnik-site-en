GTFS: a school bus stop no trip serves

Goal Read a school line's GTFS feed as published and find the stop no trip serves, with the query that proves it.

Transport authorities publish their timetables as GTFS: CSV files, stops.txt and stop_times.txt among them. Two defects pass a quick look: a temporary stop left in the file, and a stop time naming a missing stop.

  • two files, two connectors, over SFTP
  • the unserved stop found in natural language, with its SQL
  • the orphan stop time found too, through a NOT EXISTS

The GTFS feed read as published

The network drops its feed on an SFTP server. One connector per file.

  • delimiter: GTFS's comma, in UTF-8
  • a time after midnight (24:10:00) stays text and lands
  • the arret Entity: one stop per stop_id
  • the horaire Entity: one stop time per trip and rank
  1. 1
    6 stops

    stops.txt, read whole.

  2. 2
    12 stop times

    stop_times.txt, three trips.

What this step proves

A public format is read with no conversion.

A stop with no trip, a trip to a missing stop

Three questions in natural language.

  • which stops are served by no stop time: S-LYCEE-P, through a NOT IN
  • which stop times name a stop that does not exist: S1-SOIR rank 5, S-GARE-NORD, through a NOT EXISTS
  • trip S1-SOIR listed: the same rank 5

    Checkable

    data/check_gtfs.py computes the same defects, from the files alone.

  2. 2
    Declared columns

    The answer shows the card's columns only.

What this step proves

The unserved stop is found by a question, and its query can be read again.

  SQL: SELECT stop_id, stop_name, stop_lat, stop_lon
FROM (SELECT "stop_id", "stop_name", "stop_lat", "stop_lon" FROM connector."v_arret_c") AS "v_arret_c"
WHERE stop_id NOT IN (
    SELECT stop_id
    FROM (SELECT "trip_id", "stop_sequence", "arrival_time", "stop_id" FROM connector."v_horaire_c") AS "v_horaire_c"
)
LIMIT 500

- **arrival_time**: 18:08:00
- **stop_id**: S-GARE-NORD
  data as of: 2026-11-21T20:01:44.239554Z (last completed run)

  TRIP_ID  STOP_SEQUENCE  ARRIVAL_TIME  STOP_ID
  S1-SOIR  5              18:08:00      S-GARE-NORD
  1 row returned

  SQL: SELECT h.*
FROM (SELECT "trip_id", "stop_sequence", "arrival_time", "stop_id" FROM connector."v_horaire_c") AS "h"
WHERE h."stop_id" IS NOT NULL
AND NOT EXISTS (
    SELECT 1
    FROM (SELECT "stop_id", "stop_name", "stop_lat", "stop_lon" FROM connector."v_arret_c") AS "a"
    WHERE a."stop_id" = h."stop_id"
)
LIMIT 500
