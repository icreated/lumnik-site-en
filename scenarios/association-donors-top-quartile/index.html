Legacy donor software: the quarter of donors who make 76 % of an association's gifts

Goal Know which donors are in the top quarter by amount given over the year, and what share they carry.

An association exports the year's gifts, one line per gift. Everybody believes its resources rest on the many monthly direct debits. Nobody has ranked the donors by amount over the year.

  • a quartile by number of donors
  • the number of gifts beside the amount
  • codes, no names

A quartile of the donors

The export arrives over SFTP: one line per gift, the amount written with a decimal comma. The metric sums, per donor, the amount and the gifts.

  • quantile: the cut is by number of donors, not by value
  • labels [Q4, Q3, Q2, Q1]: the first label is the head, Q4 the top quarter
  • by: [donateur]: a code, no name reaches the metric
  1. 1
    By number

    A donor is in Q4 while fewer than 25 % of the donors gave more.

  2. 2
    Both measures

    The number of gifts is declared beside the amount: the same line says how the amount was given.

What this step proves

The donors' top quarter is declared in a few lines, over the export as it is.

Twenty donors, 76 % of the amount, 50 gifts

Q4 holds 20 of the 80 donors and carries 76.05 % of the year's 43,372.52 EUR.

  • 19 gave once to three times, by cheque or transfer, from 642.41 to 3,454.66 EUR
  • 1 gives by direct debit: DON-2285, 50 EUR a month
  • together: 50 gifts, 8.24 % of the year's gifts
  1. 1
    The direct debits

    528 of the 607 gifts (86.99 %) are direct debits: they make the year regular; the top quarter makes most of its amount.

  2. 2
    Checked apart

    data/check_quartile.py ranks the donors with its own code: the list and the share are the same.

What this step proves

Thanking and keeping these twenty donors is not the same work as keeping the debits running.

Limits

  • No movement yet: the donors who entered or left Q4 since last year wait for the metrics' history. One year is one total.
  • Not a loyalty score: the ranking is by amount alone; the number of gifts is shown beside, not weighed in.
  • Amounts as recorded: lumnik does not check them against the tax receipts.
  • No name: the metric holds codes; who is behind a code stays in the donor software.
  • 80 donor codes and 607 gifts, synthetic.
