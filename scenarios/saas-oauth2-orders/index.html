OAuth2: a 30-second token for a 40-second read

Goal Read a SaaS's 200 orders behind OAuth2 without losing a page when the token expires or the keys rotate, and see invalid_client when the secret is revoked.

Some orders arrive through a recent SaaS. It gives a client id and a secret, and wants a short-lived token.

  • a token renewed before it expires;
  • a key rotation absorbed mid-run;
  • a revoked secret named, never shown.

Limits

  • Client credentials only: no browser sign-in, no refresh token, no mTLS.
  • A token under 8 characters is refused: too short to be told from the data the source sends back.
  • Simulated SaaS, synthetic orders.

The problem

The token expires during the read.

  • 30 seconds of life.
  • 40 seconds of reading.

What is not changed

The SaaS stays as it is.

  • Its authorization server.
  • A client id, a secret.

The trap

A page refused in silence.

  • An expired token.
  • A key rotation mid-run.
