Four unjustified half-days in a month: the school must act

Goal Know from the monthly export which pupils reach four unjustified half-days of absence, and which pass ten, without opening the school software's report.

From four unjustified half-days in a month, the head convenes the family. Beyond ten, the academic director (Dasen) is informed. The school software counts, but the count stays in a report.

  • the monthly export arrives over SFTP
  • two Processes, one per threshold
  • one decision per pupil and threshold crossed

October's export, then two thresholds

The school software drops its monthly export on an SFTP server. October lands first, then the two rules are declared.

  • delimiter: the export's semicolon
  • demi_journees_non_justifiees>=4: the family is convened
  • demi_journees_non_justifiees>10: the Dasen is informed
  • key: eleve_id, the decision carries the pupil
  1. 1
    The software's count

    lumnik reads the figure as exported.

  2. 2
    October

    Hugo Perrin had 4: those rows precede the rules, nothing is decided.

Limits

  • lumnik does not sum half-days: the count must come from the export.
  • Pupils are minors: synthetic names. The connector's scope bounds who reads them.
  • Who is told at four differs by academy circular: the decision follows the national page.
  • No message is sent: the decision lands in the team's inbox.
  • Synthetic data.
