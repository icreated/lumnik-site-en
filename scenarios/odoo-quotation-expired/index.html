An Odoo quotation sent expired and still unanswered

Goal Be alerted to the Odoo quotation sent to a customer, past its validity date and still unanswered, without touching Odoo.

Odoo gives each quotation a validity date. The customer does not answer, the date passes, and the quotation stays "Quotation sent". Odoo shows it as expired when someone opens it, and tells no one.

  • four quotations in a real Odoo 18
  • a Workflow declares Odoo's states and the validity_date deadline
  • one decision: the only quotation sent and expired

Odoo's states and its validity date

A real Odoo 18 runs in Docker. Four quotations are created through its API. The connector reads sale_order and res_partner with a read-only login.

  • rename: id is a hub column
  • the Entity joins each quotation to its customer
  • states: draft, sent, sale, cancel, Odoo's codes
  • due.column: validity_date, Odoo's own date
  • due.states: only sent is overdue once the date has passed
  1. 1
    Nothing added

    No field is created in Odoo.

  2. 2
    Report

    The apply says the deadline is readable.

What this step proves

A deadline is declared on the date the data already carries.

Limits

  • The check runs every 15 minutes: the decision arrives within that time.
  • The sent state is written by the seed: in Odoo, "Send by email" sets it through its mail wizard, and no mail server runs here.
  • "Unanswered for 15 days" would be a dormancy, counted from lumnik's first sighting: a replay cannot wait 15 days. Odoo's validity date says the same thing, and replays.
  • Read-only: lumnik cancels, extends or re-sends no quotation.
  • Four customers and one product, synthetic.
