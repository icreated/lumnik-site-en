Apprenticeship: an OPCO installment invoiced after the contract was broken

Goal Join every OPCO invoice line to the training centre's contract register, and be told when the invoiced period runs past the contract's rupture, even when the rupture is entered after the invoice.

A business school's apprenticeship training centre (CFA), two CSV exports: 36 apprenticeship contracts, 40 OPCO invoice lines.

  • an Entity joins the contract onto every invoice line;
  • a Process compares two columns of the joined row: period_end after rupture_on;
  • two ruptures reach the register late: the invoices already sent are judged again.

The rupture on every invoice line

The Entity is keyed by the invoice line. Its join fetches the contract from the register.

  • match: invoice_line
  • joins: from contract_id, five columns of the register
  • in September, no line runs past a rupture
  1. 1
    A join, not a merge

    A join enriches the invoice lines. It never adds one.

What this step proves

A line now carries the invoiced period and its contract's rupture.

A rule on the joined line

The Process listens to the Entity's view, connector.v_cfa_invoice_a.

  • "period_end>{rupture_on}": the braces name a column, not a text
  • key: invoice_line, the decision carries the line's number
  1. 1
    Why period_end

    A period straddling the rupture already needs prorating: its end matters, not its start.

  2. 2
    No rupture, nothing

    An empty rupture_on never matches: a running contract raises nothing.

What this step proves

A rule compares two columns from two tools.

manifests/30-billed-after-rupture.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Processmetadata:  name: scenario-cfa-billed-after-rupture-aspec:  scope: cfa-billing-a  on: { event: data.row.changed, table: connector.v_cfa_invoice_a }  when:    - "period_end>{rupture_on}"        # the invoiced period runs past the contract's rupture  emit:    type: decision.billed-after-rupture-a    message: "{invoice_line}: {amount_eur} EUR invoiced to {opco} for {period_start} to {period_end}; contract {contract_id} ({apprentice_id}) broken on {rupture_on}"    key: invoice_line

Limits

  • lumnik flags the line; it computes neither the daily prorating nor the amount to repay.
  • The days actually attended, which the completion certificate requires, are not counted.
  • Training continued after the rupture is not told apart: the invoice then falls under a rectifying agreement.
  • Lines already there when the Process is applied are judged only when they or their contract change.
  • Contracts, periods and amounts are synthetic: the real schedule is each OPCO's funding agreement.

School · apprenticeship

An OPCO installment counts only for the days of the contract.

Contracts and invoices live in two tools.

36 contracts, 40 invoice lines, two CSV exports

The problem

CTR-26-0027 is broken on 9 October.

  • In November, its installment goes out anyway.
  • A rupture entered late does not catch the invoices already sent.

What is not changed

The register and the invoicing stay as they are.

  • Their usual CSV exports.
  • No development in either.

What had to be written

joins: [{ table: contracts, from: contract_id }]
when: ["period_end>{rupture_on}"]

One join, one condition between two columns.

  • An Entity: the rupture on every line.
  • A Process: one decision per line.

The rupture arrives late

Two ruptures entered at the end of October.

  • September's invoices come back up.
  • The year 1 balance, closed before, stays silent.
