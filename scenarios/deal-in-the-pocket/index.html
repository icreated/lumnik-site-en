« Traiter », then the CRM and the customer

The PWA button emits annotation.decision.acked. The automation receives the signed webhook, moves the deal to ACCORD_DE_PRINCIPE and sends the customer the summary.

  1. 1
    Correlation

    X-Lumnik-Event-Id carries the id of the decision received; the automation acts only on its handling.

  2. 2
    Signed

    The automation checks the webhook's HMAC signature.

What this step proves

The id received and the id handled match; the CRM moves on and a single email goes out.

Limits

  • The CRM, the automation and the mailbox are simulated.
  • lumnik reads the exports; it does not write to the CRM, the automation does.
  • People, companies, amounts and catalogue are fictitious.
  • The data and the PWA are French: the screenshots show the app as a French user sees it.
