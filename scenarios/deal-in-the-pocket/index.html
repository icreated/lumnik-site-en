The €240,000 deal that confirms itself during dinner

Goal Tell the sales engineer on their phone as soon as the CRM marks a deal ready to confirm, with the line, the parts and the catalogue at hand, and have one tap on Traiter move the CRM and send the customer email.

A sales engineer has dinner with Lumilait's managing director. They must answer without opening four tools:

  • the packaging line: ERP and after-sales disagree;
  • the retrofit parts: in stock or not;
  • the catalogue: 360 parts to search.

Meanwhile, the CRM marks the deal ready to confirm.

Search, then be told

One SearchIndex opens the catalogue to search.

  • titleField: description: the part's name becomes the result's title
  • match: prefix: a reference is found by its start
  • match: text: a description is found by its words (language: french)
  • facet: true: filters by family and compatible model

The App gives the PWA its name and colours. The Process watches the CRM:

  • event: data.row.changed: every changed CRM row is evaluated
  • when: status=PRET_CONFIRMATION_CLIENT: the only deal that matters
  • emit: a decision, with a readable message
  1. 1
    variateur

    30 drives, 11 for the MF-PACK 400.

  2. 2
    Webhook

    The lumi-lumilait-automation integration receives decisions and their handling.
