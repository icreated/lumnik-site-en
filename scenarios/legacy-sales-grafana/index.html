A frozen ERP feeds a real Grafana dashboard

Goal Give management an up-to-date sales dashboard from the exports of an ERP nobody touches any more.

The ERP exports its sales and its catalogue as CSV. lumnik joins them, and Grafana reads the entity through a read-only PostgreSQL account, bounded to its tenant.

Limits

  • Grafana queries PostgreSQL at an interval; this is not streaming.
  • The reading login is created for the demonstration; in production its credentials belong in a secret manager, with the same boundary.
  • The login reads tables of the connector schema: a change to the view means revisiting its grants.
  • Synthetic data.

Management wants a sales dashboard.

The ERP no longer changes.

All it can do is export its sales and catalogue as CSV.

Fictional ERP · synthetic data

The problem

Sales and products sit in two files.

  • A sale carries a product id, not its category.
  • Every morning the export replaces yesterday's.
  • Grafana does not read a changing CSV.

Nothing is changed

The ERP exports as before.

  • Two CSV files, as they are.
  • Grafana keeps its PostgreSQL source.
  • No Grafana connector to write.

What had to be written

joins:
  - table: t_legacy_bi_products_b
