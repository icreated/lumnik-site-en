Two systems, one member: each the reference for its own fields.

A mutual insurer keeps its members in its contract system and in its portal. Fused with one source order, one of the two is always wrong. lumnik lets each column name its reference source, and keeps every dispute visible until the source settles it.

Two disputes left

The contract system takes in both moves. The address disputes go; a name's case and a plan remain.

  1. 1
    Follow-up

    lm conflicts list says what is left to settle at the source.

  2. 2
    No manual step

    No correction by hand in the hub.

What this step proves

A dispute leaves the ledger when the source settles it.

Limits

  • Each column is fused on its own: preferring the portal for street and city does not tie them.
  • A preference chooses the value shown; it does not settle the dispute at the source.
  • Synthetic data.
