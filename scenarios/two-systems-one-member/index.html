Two systems and one member each one rules its own fields

Goal Show each field of a member from the system that rules it, without hiding a disagreement.

A health insurer keeps its members in its contract system and in its portal. With one source order, one of them is always wrong.

  • the address comes from the portal, the plan from the contract system;
  • every disagreement stays listed;
  • it leaves the list when the source settles it.

Limits

  • Each column is fused on its own: preferring the portal for street and city does not tie them.
  • A preference chooses the value shown; it does not settle the dispute at the source.
  • Synthetic data.

One member, two systems.

The contract system and the portal.

Six members, two nights

The problem

Who is right?

  • The contract system for the plan.
  • The portal for the address.

What is not changed

Both systems stay as they are.

  • Two exports every night.
  • No fix in the hub.

The trap

One order for everything.

  • Contract first keeps the old address.
  • Portal first loses the right plan.

What had to be written

prefer: t_scenario_member_portail_a

One line.

  • The source that rules, column by column.
  • Disagreements stay listed.
  • A 54-line scenario, three manifests.

The next night

The contract system catches up.

  • The address disagreements go away.
  • The rest are for the source to settle.

The limit

Preferring is not settling.

  • A disagreement is settled at the source.
