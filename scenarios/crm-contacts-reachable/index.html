An old CRM's contacts made reachable: names repaired, one phone, a valid email.

The sales team leaves an old CRM. Its contact export mixes capitals, accents broken by an encoding round trip, two phone columns of which one is often empty, and mistyped emails. The manifest turns it into a usable list, and names the contacts that cannot be reached.

The CRM export, transformed on the way in

In the row tier, map-columns renames CUST_NM, CONTACT_NM, TEL_MOB and TEL_FIXE; coalesce takes the mobile, else the landline. In the cell tier, fix-mojibake repairs the accents before titlecase writes the names; parse-email lower-cases and validates the email; parse-phone writes the number in international format, assuming France. on-error sends a contact whose email cannot be read to the dead letters.
