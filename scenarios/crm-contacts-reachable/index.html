The CRM export, transformed on the way in

Two stages: the row, then the cells.

  • map-columns: CUST_NM, CONTACT_NM, TEL_MOB… get readable names
  • coalesce: the mobile, else the landline, in one phone column
  • fix-mojibake: BÃ©nÃ©dicte becomes Bénédicte again
  • titlecase: DUPONT SARL becomes Dupont Sarl
  • parse-email: lower case, and refuses an unreadable email
  • parse-phone: international format
  • on-error: an unreadable email sends the contact to the dead letters
  1. 1
    Why fix-mojibake before titlecase

    Repair the accents first: otherwise Ã‰ would be cased as a word.

  2. 2
    Why default-region: FR

    Without it, 06 12 34 56 78 would be read as a US number.
