PrestaShop: the whole catalogue in one line, and lumnik sees when the shop keeps sending the same page.

Goal Read every product of a PrestaShop 8 shop through its Webservice, without touching the shop.

A real PrestaShop 8.2.8 and its demo catalogue, read through the Webservice.

  • it answers XML: the hub refuses it by name;
  • in JSON, it ignores offset=: the same page comes back, and the hub refuses it instead of reading it 10 000 times;
  • pagination: { kind: prestashop }: the 19 products, limit=0,5 then limit=5,5.

PrestaShop 8.2.8, its demo catalogue

The problem

PrestaShop has its habits.

  • It answers XML by default.
  • It pages its own way: limit=0,50.

What is not changed

The shop stays as it is.

  • Its Webservice, a read-only key.
  • No module added.

The trap

A pagination ignored.

  • The same page comes back on every call.
  • The hub sees it and refuses, instead of reporting success.

What had to be written

pagination: { kind: prestashop }

Name the software.

  • 19 products out of 19.
  • A 23-line connector.

What was fixed

Two gaps found, two fixes.

  • A repeated page fails the read.
  • The prestashop preset.

The limit

The hub reads the Webservice's JSON.

  • XML is still refused, by name.
  • No total: the read stops on a short page.
