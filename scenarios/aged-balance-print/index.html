A printed aged balance: every invoice with its client, from one page to the next.

The accounting system prints its aged balance to a spool file: a header on each page, then for each client a line with its account and name, its open invoices below, and a total. A client's invoices can run onto the next page. Credit control wants each invoice with its client.

A printed report, without its layout

lines: true reads each line of the report. filter-where keeps the client headers and the invoices only: page headers, totals and the grand total are dropped and counted. regex-extract cuts the account, name, piece, dates, balance and days late.

The whole balance, the printed totals found again

Every invoice has its client. Each client's invoices add up to its printed total: 4,762.95, 8,957.30 and 5,815.05. Two invoices are over 60 days late, both at 411ANSE.

  1. 1
    85 and 77 days

    FA26-0644 and FA26-0708.

What this step proves

Credit control reads its overdue invoices without retyping the report.

Limits

  • A line dropped by filter-where carries nothing: the filter must keep the client headers.
  • The entity is keyed by the printed line: an invoice reprinted identically is the same row.
  • Synthetic file, fictitious clients and invoices.
