A technician sees only the maintenance app

Goal Give each business line its app on the same hub, without a technician being able to read purchasing's data.

Maintenance and purchasing share an ERP where everyone sees everything. On the same hub, lumnik composes one app per business line.

  • the technician holds only the Keycloak role scope:maintenance
  • they see their app, their orders and their decisions
  • every purchasing door answers with a refusal

Two métiers on one hub

The integrator declares, for each business line, a connector, an entity, a view, an app and a Process. Every manifest carries the line's scope.

  • maintenance: the work orders
  • purchasing: the supplier invoices, two decisions above 10,000
  1. 1
    Maintenance

    Work orders; an open urgent order becomes a decision.

  2. 2
    Purchasing

    Supplier invoices; an open invoice above 10,000 becomes a decision.

A métier is a declared composition, not a copy of the ERP.

Limits

  • The métier is a Keycloak role scope:<tag>; a user with no scope: role at all sees the whole tenant.
  • An unknown entity answers 404, another métier's 403: names can be told apart, never rows.
  • The scenario goes through lm; the PWA reads the same doors.
  • Synthetic data.

The problem

Opening the data opens it to all.

  • One screen per business line is not enough.
  • A direct query bypasses the screen.
  • A known id opens someone else's decision.

Nothing is changed

The ERP and Keycloak stay in place.

  • The business line is a Keycloak role.
  • lumnik reads it, it does not manage it.
  • No application to build.

What they do not see

Every purchasing door refuses.

Real answer, replay of 3 November 2026, Keycloak OIDC.

The limit

With no scope role, a user sees the whole tenant.

  • The business line is set in Keycloak.
  • An unknown entity answers 404, another line's 403.
  • Names can be told apart, never rows.
