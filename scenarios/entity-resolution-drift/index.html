The email changes; the link does not move

The CRM delivers CC-42 with Helios Valves' email. The run is enough: CC-42 becomes ambiguous between Nova Pumps and Helios Valves.

    Refusal

    Trace

    The history keeps yesterday's link, then the ambiguity.
