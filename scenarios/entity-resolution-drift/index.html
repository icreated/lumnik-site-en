Limits

  • The scenario guards against a move; it does not say why the CRM changed.
  • Assertions and source cursors are durable: a full replay needs a new instance.
  • Synthetic data.

Customers · ERP and CRM

A contact already tied to its customer.

The CRM changes its email.

Two accounts, one contact, two days

The problem

The email points at another customer.

  • Yesterday: Nova Pumps.
  • Today: Helios Valves.

What is not changed

The sources stay as they are.

  • One CRM file a day.
  • No fix by hand.

The trap

Moving in silence.

  • The contact switches customer.
  • Nobody sees it.

What had to be written

normalize: [lower]

One line.

  • One rule: the email.
  • An established link does not move alone.
  • A 51-line scenario, three manifests.

The decision

A human decides.

  • CC-42 stays on Nova Pumps.
  • The history names the author and the reason.

The limit

The hub does not know why the CRM changed.

  • It shows it, it does not explain it.
