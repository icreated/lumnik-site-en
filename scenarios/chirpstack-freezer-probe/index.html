The freezer probe reads 6.8 °C in the middle of the night

Goal Be alerted as soon as an independent HACCP probe sees a freezer above -18 °C, even when the refrigeration controller says nothing.

In autumn 2026, supermarket refrigeration controllers were changed remotely. In France, on 11 October, no alarm went off. An independent HACCP probe measures the temperature, and ChirpStack publishes its readings to Kafka.

  • the reading is nested in the event: promote turns it into a column
  • a registry says which cabinet, which aisle, who is on call
  • at 02:30, PROBE-07 reads 6.8 °C: one decision

Readings on Kafka, cabinets in CSV

ChirpStack publishes every LoRaWAN event to Kafka. The CSV registry says which cabinet each probe measures.

  • the temperature is nested: object.temperature
  • promote reads it into a decimal column
  • status events arrive on the same topic, with no temperature

    A ChirpStack v4 up event, as JSON.

Above -18 °C, a decision

A Process reads the promoted column on the raw table. Above -18 °C, it emits a decision.

  • when: temperature_c>-18
  • a reading with no temperature emits nothing
  1. 1
    Raw table

    The Process reads the event's columns, not the entity's.

  2. 2
    Status

    A status event has no temperature: it fails the condition.

What this step proves

A numeric condition applies to the nested value.

Limits

  • lumnik sees only what the probe sends: a silent probe, gateway or ChirpStack raises no decision here.
  • The decision message cites the event's columns only: the cabinet and on-call are on the record, not in the message.
  • One threshold, -18 °C, for every cabinet of this Process.
  • lumnik does not block an attack and does not run the cooling.
  • ChirpStack publishes every event type on the same topic: a status arrives as a row with no temperature.
  • Fictional store, synthetic data.

The problem

The alarm lives in the box under attack.

  • The controller runs the cooling and its own alarms.
  • A HACCP probe measures separately, over its own radio.
  • Its reading arrives nested in a ChirpStack event.

Nothing is changed

The probe, ChirpStack and Kafka stay in place.

  • The topic stays ChirpStack's.
  • The cabinet registry stays a CSV file.
  • No application to build.
