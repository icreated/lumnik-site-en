Four EDI files for a single order line

Goal Merge the four flows to know whether each line is ordered, confirmed or delivered, and be alerted when a confirmed date passes without delivery.

A manufacturer and its logistics partner exchange four flat files around one order line: the order, the acknowledgement, the confirmed date, the delivery note. Sales administration asks, line by line:

  • never acknowledged?
  • confirmed but not delivered?
  • delivered in another quantity?

Four flows, four layouts

One connector per flow, fixed widths.

  • lines: true: each file line arrives whole, in a line column
  • filter-where: skips the header, which carries no customer code
  • regex-extract: cuts the fields by their width
  • enrich-context: stage: each flow says the stage it attests
  1. 1
    Stage

    ORDERED, CONFIRMED, DELIVERED: one per flow.

  2. 2
    Acknowledgement

    It carries only the purchase order, attached to the order.

Each line, at its furthest stage

The Entity fuses the flows by line_id.

  • sources: the order is the priority: delivery note, then confirmed date, then order
  • joins: the acknowledgement joins by order, not by line
  • conflicts: false on stage: the stage progresses, it is not a disagreement
  1. 1
    45 for 50

    6880011 delivered in another quantity, side by side.

  2. 2
    Why conflicts: false

    Without it, three flows naming three stages would be three disagreements.

The line's lifecycle, declared on the fused view

The Workflow follows the view's stage column.

  • key: line_id: one observation per line
  • states: ORDERED, CONFIRMED, DELIVERED
  • due: the deadline is the confirmed date, for a line still CONFIRMED
  • zone: Europe/Paris: the date falls at the end of that day, Paris time

    dueProblem

Limits

  • The date reads at the end of the day, Paris time. A confirmed date moved to another past day raises a new alert. A date already reported, no.
  • A different delivered quantity shows side by side. No filter compares two columns.
  • Synthetic files, on the template of a real exchange.

Where does this order line stand?

The problem

Sales admin opens four files for one answer.

  • Never acknowledged?
  • Confirmed but not delivered?
  • Delivered in another quantity?

Nothing is changed

The files stay as they are.

  • lumnik reads the four flows.
  • It matches them line by line.
  • Each line takes its furthest stage.

What had to be written

due:
  column: confirmed_date
  states: [CONFIRMED]

The confirmed date becomes a deadline.

The whole scenario fits in 6 declaration files, 144 lines.

The answers

Each line, its stage.

  • 6880012: confirmed date, not delivered.
  • 6880021: never acknowledged.
  • 6880011: delivered 45 for 50.

The delay

The date has passed. The line is not delivered.

Real message, replay of 28 October 2026.

The limit

lumnik shows the gap. Sales admin judges it.

  • 45 for 50 reads side by side.
  • No filter compares two columns.
