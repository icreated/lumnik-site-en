SAP OData v2: the 48,000 EUR order left unprocessed

Goal Read SAP's sales orders through the OData v2 Gateway, then be told when a large order stays unprocessed.

SAP's Sales Order API, API_SALES_ORDER_SRV, served the way a Gateway serves it: October's 130 orders.

  • odata-v2: three $skiptoken pages, the __count total checked;
  • from_odata_date: the 130 /Date()/ values become dates;
  • 48,000 EUR in status A: one decision, then a dormancy alert.

130 orders behind a Gateway

No SAP system fits in a container. The lab runs SAP's CAP framework and its OData v2 adapter: they answer the way a Gateway does, under /sap/opu/odata/sap/API_SALES_ORDER_SRV.

  • __count: the total, as text
  • __next: the next page, with $skiptoken
  • /Date(…)/: OData v2's date format
  1. 1
    Everything as text

    The total ("130") and the amounts ("247.25") arrive as text: the hub reads them anyway.

The 130 orders in three pages

The odata-v2 preset reads d.results, follows d.__next and checks the d.__count total.

  • $format=json&$inlinecount=allpages: JSON and the total, asked for in path:
  • from_odata_date: /Date(1791763200000+0000)/ becomes 2026-10-01
  1. 1
    Before the fix

    The 130 delivery dates landed empty, with 130 cell errors. No rule could read a deadline.

A decision for a large unprocessed order

The Process listens on the REST connector's table.

  • status=A: not processed yet
  • net_amount>=10000: October's orders stay below 2,000 EUR
  • key: sales_order, the decision carries the order number
  1. 1
    Applied before the order is keyed in

    A Process does not evaluate the rows already there: only new rows, and rows that change at the source.

What this step proves

A business rule is declared on the columns read from SAP.

Unprocessed: the order sleeps

The Workflow follows OverallSDProcessStatus, the status SAP writes. On the second sweep, the order has stayed in A past the threshold.

  • key: sales_order, one order watched per number
  • dormancy.after: 1m for the replay; a sales desk would write 1d
  • C: terminal, a shipped order never sleeps
  1. 1
    The sweep

    It runs every 15 minutes: the full replay takes up to 35 minutes.

What this step proves

A forgotten order becomes an alert, with no manual reminder.

Limits

  • The lab is SAP CAP and its OData v2 adapter, not an ECC or S/4HANA system: the answers have a Gateway's format.
  • The offset in /Date(…+0060)/ is not applied: the milliseconds are already UTC.
  • odata-v2 sends no page size: the Gateway's 50 per page governs the run.
  • The dormancy counts from the first time lumnik sees the status, not from the order's creation in SAP.
  • Synthetic orders, customers and amounts.

Sales · SAP

The orders are in SAP.

They must be read without a manual export.

API_SALES_ORDER_SRV, OData v2, 130 orders

The problem

A 48,000 EUR order stays in status A.

  • October's orders stay below 2,000 EUR.
  • This one is not processed.

What is not changed

SAP stays as it is.

  • The OData v2 service SAP already exposes.
  • No ABAP development, no export.

What had to be written

pagination: { kind: odata-v2 }
transform: from_odata_date

One pagination line, one transform.

  • A Process: one decision for the order.
  • A Workflow: an alert when it sleeps.

What was fixed

SAP dates used to land empty.

  • /Date(ms)/ is read as a date (#1826).
  • The odata-v2 preset names the pagination (#1827).
