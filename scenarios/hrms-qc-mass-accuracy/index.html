LC-HRMS: the samples acquired after an out-of-tolerance QC

Goal Read the QC export of an LC-HRMS screening without losing a decimal, and flag every sample acquired after a QC outside its mass tolerance, to know which ones to reinject.

An environmental laboratory screens micropollutants in water on an Orbitrap. The transients and spectra stay on the instrument; lumnik reads what the processing software exports.

  • a key;value block, a header over two lines, n.d. and < LOD in the intensity column;
  • every m/z kept with its five decimals;
  • the air conditioning fails: the QC reads 3.84 ppm, the four samples after it raise a decision.

The QC export of the morning batch

The processing software writes one file per batch.

  • a key;value block: batch, instrument, method, calibration
  • the header over two lines: the names, then the units
  • one line per target ion; the injection and its QC summary on its first line only
  1. 1
    Computed masses

    Every theoretical m/z comes from the monoisotopic atomic masses and the electron mass: caffeine [M+H]+, 195.08765. Every Δm is recomputed from the written value.

  2. 2
    Resolving power

    70,000 FWHM at m/z 200, falling as 1/√(m/z): about 70,900 on caffeine, 40,100 on reserpine.

61 ion lines, every m/z with its five decimals

The csv-file connector, semicolon-separated.

  • short_rows: skip-leading: the key;value block is skipped, counted, never a dead letter
  • filter-where: the two header lines carry no m/z
  • fill-down: the injection onto each of its ions, the last QC onto the samples after it
  • n.d. and < LOD go into detection, the intensity stays a number
  1. 1
    n.d. is not < LOD

    n.d.: no peak in the window. < LOD: a peak found, below the limit of detection; its measured m/z is kept.

What this step proves

An exact mass reaches the hub as the instrument wrote it.

manifests/10-qc-export.yaml

Acquired after a QC out of tolerance

The Process listens to the connector's table.

  • type=Sample, on the injection's first line: one decision per sample, not per compound
  • qc_max_ppm>3: the batch's acceptance, |Δm| <= 3.0 ppm on every QC ion
  1. 1
    The worst ion

    The software's QC summary gives the largest |Δm| of the three ions: one ion out of tolerance is enough.

What this step proves

A laboratory's acceptance rule is written in three conditions.

manifests/20-after-a-failed-qc.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Processmetadata:  name: scenario-hrms-qc-aspec:  scope: qc-hrms-a  on: { event: data.row.changed, table: connector.t_scenario_hrms_a }  when:    - type=Sample    - inj>0                          # the injection's first line: one decision per sample, not per compound    - qc_max_ppm>3                   # the QC acceptance: |Δm| <= 3.0 ppm on every calibrant ion  emit:    type: decision.after-failed-qc-a    message: "{sample} (injection {inj}): the QC before it reads {qc_max_ppm} ppm, acceptance 3.0 ppm"    key: sample

The air conditioning fails: four samples to reinject

The instrument drifts by about +3.6 ppm. The second QC reads 3.84 ppm.

  • W-26-0419 to 0422, acquired after it: four decisions
  • the operator recalibrates: the QC is forgotten, W-26-0423 and 0424 are covered by no QC
  • the next QC reads 0.25 ppm: W-26-0425 and 0426 are accepted
  1. 1
    The drift shows on the samples too

    Their own Δm run from 3.2 to 4.2 ppm: the QC's drift, on ions from m/z 216 to 296.

Limits

  • The rule reads the preceding QC, not the bracket: W-26-0417 and 0418, acquired before the failed QC while the drift built up, reach 2.6 and 3.3 ppm and are not flagged.
  • A sample acquired after a recalibration and before the next QC is covered by no QC; the page shows it, no decision says it.
  • Only the mass error is judged. The QC's resolving power and intensity are read; no rule judges them here.
  • A lost identification caused by the drift (a false n.d.) is not detected: the decision only says not to trust the sample.
  • Samples, intensities and drift synthetic; the theoretical masses are exact.

Laboratory · HRMS

A QC standard between each series of samples.

Caffeine, MRFA, reserpine: their mass error tells whether the samples after them can be trusted.

195.08765 · 524.26496 · 609.28066

The problem

The QC reads 3.84 ppm. The batch goes on.

  • The tolerance is 3.0 ppm.
  • Four samples are acquired after it, as if nothing happened.

What is not changed

The instrument and its software stay as they are.

  • The export is the processing software's own.
  • No spectrum leaves the instrument.

What had to be written

fill-down: { columns: [qc_max_ppm],
  reset-when: { column: type, in: [Cal] } }

The last QC speaks for the samples after it.

  • Until the recalibration.
  • One rule: qc_max_ppm>3.

What was fixed

The hub kept four decimals (#1862).

  • 195.08765 became 195.0877: 0.26 ppm of added error.
  • The header block is no longer a dead letter (#1863).
