Firebird: a Delphi application's articles under their stock threshold

Goal Read a Delphi application's articles through the isql export of its Firebird database, and be told when an article falls under its reorder threshold.

A Delphi stock application on Firebird 5: 40 articles, a WIN1252 database.

  • jdbc-generic has no Firebird dialect: the apply refuses, and says what is installed;
  • the export the shop already schedules, isql, is read by csv-file;
  • a day of sales: three articles under their threshold, three reorder decisions.

Firebird, read directly: refused

The first attempt: jdbc-generic, as for PostgreSQL. The apply refuses before any connection.

  • no installed dialect accepts jdbc:firebirdsql
  • schema is required, and Firebird has no schemas
  1. 1
    A refusal that says why

    The message names the dialects this hub carries. A Firebird provider is written when a customer needs one.

What this step proves

A database the hub cannot read is refused at apply, never half-read.

The export the shop already schedules

isql, Firebird's own client, writes the articles to a share, in the database's character set.

  • WIN1252: é is the byte E9
  • every line padded to the expression's width
  • the header padded too: the hub trims its names (#1853)
  1. 1
    OUTPUT appends

    isql adds to an existing file. The job removes yesterday's export first.

What this step proves

The database leaves through a gesture the shop already knows.

A threshold per article

Each article carries its own reorder threshold, SEUIL. The Process compares the two columns of the row.

  • "qte_stock<{seuil}": braces name a column
  • key: ref, the decision carries the article reference
  1. 1
    No fixed number

    The threshold stays in the Delphi application, where the shop sets it.

What this step proves

A rule reads the threshold the business already maintains.

manifests/30-below-threshold.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Processmetadata:  name: scenario-fb-reorder-aspec:  scope: stock-a  on: { event: data.row.changed, table: connector.t_scenario_fb_articles_a }  when:    - "qte_stock<{seuil}"            # the stock fell under the article's own threshold  emit:    type: decision.reorder-a    message: "{ref} {libelle}: {qte_stock} in stock, threshold {seuil}"    key: ref

Limits

  • This hub carries the oracle and postgres dialects (Pro); an Open hub lists postgres alone.
  • The export is a daily file: a sale is seen at the next export, not at the till.
  • Synthetic articles, stocks and prices.

Stock · Delphi

The stock lives in a Delphi application.

Its database is Firebird.

40 articles, WIN1252, Firebird 5

The refusal

No Firebird dialect.

  • The apply refuses, and names what is installed.
  • Until a customer needs one, the export does.

What is not changed

The Delphi application stays as it is.

  • The isql export it already schedules.
  • WIN1252 accents, padded lines.

What had to be written

encoding: windows-1252
when: ["qte_stock<{seuil}"]

One encoding, one condition between two columns.

  • csv-file: the export as it comes.
  • A Process: one decision per article.

What was found

A padded header became a column name.

  • The hub now trims it (#1853).
  • isql's OUTPUT appends: the job removes the file first.
