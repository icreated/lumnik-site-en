The CRM's SOAP desk read service by service with nothing read twice

Goal Read an old CRM's SOAP services, each as it reads, without hammering it.

A 2000s CRM exposes its data through custom SOAP services. The password travels in the envelope.

  • one service read in one call, another page by page;
  • the CRM's Fault on the run, with no retry;
  • the next night updated on the keys.

Limits

  • A SOAP read is one pass, in memory: lm run cancel only takes effect when the read ends.
  • The service is called for one case and one customer written in the manifest; one call per case of a list is not covered.
  • An incremental import's mark travels in the URL, not in the body: a service that filters by period in its body is read whole.
  • Simulated CRM; synthetic data.

CRM · SOAP

The CRM speaks SOAP.

Custom services, from the 2000s.

Simulated CRM, two services

The problem

Each service reads its own way.

  • One returns everything in one call.
  • Another pages by page number.

What is not changed

The CRM stays as it is.

  • Its services, its envelope.
  • A service account.

The trap

Hammering the CRM.

  • Reading the same page in a loop.
  • Retrying a refused password.

What had to be written

<data:page>{{page}}</data:page>

One line.

  • The page number, where the service expects it.
  • The stop on the last page.
  • An 84-line scenario, two manifests.

A refusal

The CRM's Fault, verbatim.

  • On the run, with its code.
  • A single attempt.

The limit

One request, one client, written in the manifest.

  • One call per item of a list is not covered.
