Key=value pairs for what comes next

A jq -r | while read turns the production inventory into service=… owner=… deployed=… approved=… lines for the next DevOps step.

  1. 1
    Unix

    No dedicated client: jq, tee and the shell.

  2. 2
    Two services

    billing-api and catalog-api, aligned.

What this step proves

lm's JSON is consumed with standard tools.

Limits

  • The gate compares declared versions; it does not check the image actually running.
  • The inventory is true at its collection time: the gate reads the latest observation, not the cluster's instant state.
  • Synthetic data.
