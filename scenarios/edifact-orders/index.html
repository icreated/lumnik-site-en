An EDIFACT ORDERS message: one line, split into segments, the escaped apostrophe kept.

Retail and industry still exchange EDIFACT: a buyer's ORDERS message, often written on one single line, its segments ended by an apostrophe, its elements split by + and :. An apostrophe in a label is written ?'. The buyer wants to read the message segment by segment.

One line, split into segments

lines: true reads the message as one line. split-row cuts it on the apostrophe, with escape: "?" so that ?' stays an apostrophe, and position numbers the segments in message order. regex-extract separates the segment's tag from its elements.

A one-line message becomes fourteen segments, no phantom one.

One order line, two segments

A filter on the tag brings out the two LIN and the two QTY. Each QTY follows its LIN, but on its own row: lumnik does not yet carry the LIN's EAN, nor the BGM's number, onto the segments that follow.

What this step proves

The reading is done; the pairing is next.

Limits

  • An order line "order, EAN, quantity" cannot be rebuilt yet: a segment's value must be carried onto the following ones (#1452).
  • The UNA segment, which declares the separators, goes through the same escape: it reads ":+.".
  • One message per file; fictitious GLN and EAN numbers.
