One product record, two databases: PostgreSQL and Oracle stay where they are.

Products live in PostgreSQL, their categories in Oracle. Two jdbc connectors read them without writing to either, and one entity joins them: 40,234 products, each with its category when there is one.

Both database passwords go into lm secret

The manifest names the secret with password_env; the value goes through stdin, never through argv or the manifest.

  1. 1
    Order

    The hub looks in its own environment first, then in lm secret.

  2. 2
    No restart

    No variable to add to the hub: the secret is enough.

What this step proves

A jdbc connector written as a manifest gets its password without touching the hub.

PostgreSQL and Oracle, read only

40,234 products in PostgreSQL, 63 categories in Oracle Free 23ai, through the embedded ojdbc17 driver.

  1. 1
    Oracle names

    rename brings the Oracle columns to lower case; target names the hub table.

  2. 2
    Conservation

    IN = OUT for both sources: no row set aside.

What this step proves

Both databases are read in full, each through its own dialect.

The product carries its Oracle category

MergedProductB joins the PostgreSQL products to their Oracle categories on m_product_category_id.

  1. 1
    Business key

    The category id is text on both sides: an Oracle NUMBER would not print like a PostgreSQL integer.

  2. 2
    Left join

    A product without a category stays in the entity.

What this step proves

Two databases from two vendors become one record, declared in a manifest.

manifests/30-product-with-category.yaml
kind: Entityname: MergedProductBdescription: Products from PostgreSQL enriched with their Oracle category.match: [product_id]scopes: [catalogue]sources:  - table: t_m_product_b    map:      m_product_id: product_id      m_product_category_id: category_id      name: product_name      description: product_description      isactive: product_activejoins:  - table: t_m_product_category_b    from: category_id    to: m_product_category_id    map:      name: category_name      description: category_description      isactive: category_active
$ lm entity apply -f manifests/30-product-with-category.yaml

Every product, exactly once

Two products of the Pompes category both read it; the sentinel product without a category stays, with null fields.

  1. 1
    Cardinality

    40,234 rows in the entity, as many as in PostgreSQL.

  2. 2
    Sharing

    One category enriches several products without merging them.

What this step proves

The join keeps every product and duplicates none.
