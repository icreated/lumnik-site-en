The product in PostgreSQL and its category in Oracle

Goal Tie each PostgreSQL product to its Oracle category, read-only, without losing or duplicating a product.

Products live in PostgreSQL, their categories in Oracle. Two jdbc connectors read them without writing anything, and an entity joins them.

  • 40,234 products, 63 categories
  • each product carries its category when there is one
  • the product with no category stays, with empty fields

PostgreSQL and Oracle, read only

Two jdbc connectors read both databases, read-only.

  • PostgreSQL: 40,234 products
  • Oracle Free 23ai: 63 categories, through the embedded ojdbc17 driver
  1. 1
    Oracle names

    rename brings the Oracle columns to lower case; target names the hub table.

  2. 2
    Conservation

    IN = OUT for both sources: no row set aside.

What this step proves

Both databases are read in full, each through its own dialect.

The product carries its Oracle category

MergedProductB joins each PostgreSQL product to its Oracle category.

  • key: m_product_category_id
  1. 1
    Business key

    The category id is text on both sides: an Oracle NUMBER would not print like a PostgreSQL integer.

  2. 2
    Left join

    A product without a category stays in the entity.

What this step proves

Two databases from two vendors become one record, declared in a manifest.

manifests/30-product-with-category.yaml
kind: Entityname: MergedProductBdescription: Products from PostgreSQL enriched with their Oracle category.match: [product_id]scopes: [catalogue]sources:  - table: t_m_product_b    map:      m_product_id: product_id      m_product_category_id: category_id      name: product_name      description: product_description      isactive: product_activejoins:  - table: t_m_product_category_b    from: category_id    to: m_product_category_id    map:      name: category_name      description: category_description      isactive: category_active

Catalogue · two databases

The product is in PostgreSQL.

Its category is in Oracle.

Two legacy databases, neither sees the other.

Synthetic catalogue

The problem

A product record needs both.

  • Migrating a database is a project.
  • A home-made ETL is one more script.
  • A failed join makes products disappear.

Nothing is migrated

Both databases stay where they are.

  • Two read-only accounts.
  • The passwords in lm secret.
  • No application to build.

What had to be written

joins:
  - table: t_m_product_category_b

Replay of 3 November 2026.

Nothing disappears

A product with no category stays.

  • 9999999 has no category in Oracle.
  • It is on the record, category empty.
  • The join is a left join.

The limit

Full mode does not see a row changed in place.

  • It reads what follows the last key.
  • To follow updates: a watermark column.
  • Oracle is Pro edition only.
