Ask refuses them instead of inventing a figure someone would repeat in a meeting.

Three questions, three figures, the SQL beside them

How many orders were cancelled, how much was ordered from SUP-003 outside cancellations, which SUP-003 orders exceed 15,000 euros. Each answer shows the SQL that ran and its rows. A sentence restating the question's threshold is kept (#1391); a figure neither the rows nor the query back would be withheld.

  1. 1
    26 cancelled

    COUNT(*) on the cancelled status.

  2. 2
    7 orders

    Over 15,000 euros, from SUP-003.

A margin, a best supplier, delays: refused

No column holds a cost, a criterion for "best" or a delivery date. Ask runs no query and says so: the question does not map to the declared schema. In JSON, abstained is true and the SQL is empty; a script reads that without interpreting the sentence.

An absent supplier: "no rows" is not "no orders"

SUP-009 appears on no order. The query runs and returns nothing. Ask does not conclude there are no orders: it says the query may have missed the question, and asks to check the SQL.

What this step proves

An empty result is never phrased as a negative answer.

Limits

  • The refusals come from the model configured on the hub (qwen2.5-coder:7b here). Another model may translate a question this one refuses; its figures then go through the same checks.
  • A date restated in the answer is recognised by its year only: "since 15 March" withholds the sentence, not the rows.
  • Synthetic orders; one entity, one scope.
