Limits

  • The refusals come from the model configured on the hub (qwen2.5-coder:7b here). Another model may translate a question this one refuses; its figures then go through the same checks.
  • A date restated in the answer is recognised by its year only: "since 15 March" withholds the sentence, not the rows.
  • Synthetic orders; one entity, one scope.
