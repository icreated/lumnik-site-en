The catalogue reaches the partner in their format, not ours.

Two ERP CSV files, products and categories, are joined in the hub and delivered to S3 in a partner's format: French headers, semicolons, decimal commas, recoded statuses, a masked internal reference. A receipt proves the delivered bytes.

The partner's format is declared, not coded

The Export renames the columns, formats the version and the date, recodes statuses, masks the category reference and keeps active categories only.

  1. 1
    S3 keys

    Both keys go through stdin into hub secrets, never into argv or the manifest.

  2. 2
    Receipt

    The hub records the row count, the byte count and the SHA-256 of the delivered object.

What this step proves

The delivered bytes are the receipt's

The runner reads the object back from MinIO and checks it against the receipt: 38,300 rows, same size, same SHA-256.

  1. 1
    Filter

    The 1,933 products of inactive categories are not sent.

  2. 2
    Mask

    REF_CATEGORIE shows only its last four digits.

What this step proves

What the partner receives matches, byte for byte, what the hub recorded.

Limits

  • Masking a predictable identifier is pseudonymisation, not encryption; if the partner must not correlate categories, drop the column.
  • Snapshot mode rewrites the whole object on every run.
  • Synthetic data.
