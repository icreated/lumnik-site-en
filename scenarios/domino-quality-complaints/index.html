Lotus Notes: the critical complaints with no owner

Goal Read the open complaints of a Lotus Notes view through the Domino Data Service, and be told when a critical complaint has no owner.

A valve manufacturer's quality department has tracked its customer complaints in a Lotus Notes database for twenty years. The Domino Data Service exposes it as JSON.

  • start and count, the view's count in Content-Range;
  • a multi-value field: a string with one name, a list with several;
  • two critical complaints with no owner, two decisions.

The view OpenComplaints, as a Reader

The Domino Data Service exposes the views of a Notes database.

  • GET /quality/complaints.nsf/api/data/collections/name/OpenComplaints

57 complaints, three pages, the count checked

The rest-generic connector, offset pagination.

  • start and count: Domino's names
  • total_header: Content-Range, items 0-19/57: the view counts 57
  • unique_key: @unid, the document's id across every replica
  1. 1
    A multi-value field

    Owner is a string with one name, a list with several. The list is kept as Domino wrote it, canonical names included.

What this step proves

A Notes database is read with no agent, no export, no change to its design.

Critical, and nobody on it

The Process listens to the connector's ext.* table.

  • severity=1 - Critique
  • assignment=Non attribuée: the view's column $7, @If(Owner = ""; "Non attribuée"; "Attribuée")
  1. 1
    The formula stays in Notes

    The rule reads the view's verdict. Nobody rewrites the @formula outside Notes.

What this step proves

A rule sits on a column Notes computes itself.

manifests/20-critical-unassigned.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Processmetadata:  name: scenario-domino-critical-aspec:  scope: quality-a  on: { event: data.row.changed, table: ext.scenario_domino_a }  when:    - severity=1 - Critique    - assignment=Non attribuée  emit:    type: decision.critical-unassigned-a    message: "{complaint_no}: critical complaint from {customer} on {product}, received {received}, nobody assigned"    key: complaint_no

Limits

  • Replayed against a stand-in for the Domino Data Service written for this scenario, not a Domino server: its headers, its page past the end and its count ceiling are not verified here.
  • The view is read whole on every run: nothing from Domino says here what changed since the last read.
  • A categorized view (category rows, response documents at @position 1.2) was not replayed.
  • Session authentication (Domino's login form, the LtpaToken cookie) was not replayed: HTTP Basic only.
  • Complaints, customers and names synthetic.

The problem

A critical complaint arrives in the evening. It waits for the morning.

  • The Owner field is empty.
  • The view shows it to whoever opens it.

What is not changed

The Notes database stays as it is.

  • Its forms, its views, its ACL.
  • A Notes user with Reader access.

What had to be written

offset_param: start
limit_param: count
total_header: Content-Range

Domino's pagination, declared.

  • The view's count checks the read.
  • The computed column $7 says the assignment.

What was fixed

Content-Range reads as a count (#1866).

  • items 0-19/57: 57.
  • A list promoted as text no longer escapes its / (#1867).
