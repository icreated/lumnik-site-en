A closed factory still drawing power at night

Goal Be alerted when a closed site draws more than its night threshold, without alerting the sites where that load is normal.

  • the laboratory draws 28.4 kW, under its 40 kW threshold: nothing

One night threshold per site

Three kind:Process read the raw Kafka table, one per site. Only the factory's decision is routed by email.

  • factory: above 10 kW, site closed
  • office: above 3 kW
  • laboratory: above 40 kW

Limits

  • A policy sees only the Kafka reading's columns: the owner and context from the CSV cannot enter the decision message in v1.
  • The recipient is a static rule for this decision type; lumnik does not read the address from the entity.
  • lumnik compares a measurement with a declared threshold; it does not diagnose the cause. period=CLOSED comes from the source.
  • Grafana queries postgres every 5 seconds; this is not streaming.
  • Synthetic data.

Energy · three sites

At 23:15, a closed factory draws power.

Lyon draws 18.7 kW.

At the same moment, the Grenoble laboratory draws 28.4 kW.

Fictional sites · synthetic data

The problem

The same number does not mean the same thing everywhere.

  • The meter sends a site code and kW.
  • The CSV registry gives the site type and its night threshold.
  • Neither one knows a threshold is crossed.

Nothing is changed

The meters, Kafka and the file stay in place.

  • The Kafka producer does not change.
  • The CSV registry stays where it is.
  • No application to build.

What had to be written

when:
  - site_id=SITE-LYON
  - period=CLOSED
  - power_kw>10

One night threshold per site.

A 110-line scenario, six declaration files.

What the owner receives

One email, for Lyon only.

Real message, replay of 3 November 2026.

Silence is an answer too

The laboratory runs all night.

  • 28.4 kW, against a 40 kW threshold.
  • Its climate chambers never stop.
  • No decision, no email.

The limit

lumnik compares a measurement with a declared threshold.

  • It does not say why Lyon draws power.
  • “Site closed” comes from the source, not a calendar.
  • The owner comes from the CSV, not the message.
