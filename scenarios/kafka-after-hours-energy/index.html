One night threshold per site

Three kind:Process watch the raw Kafka table: factory above 10 kW, office above 3 kW, laboratory above 40 kW, site closed. The factory's decision goes by email to its owner.

  1. 1
    Exact rule

    notify add routes the factory's decision type alone to a declared address.

  2. 2
    Raw table

    A policy reads the Kafka reading's columns, not the entity's.

What this step proves

The same number means something different under each site's declared policy.

Limits

  • A policy sees only the Kafka reading's columns: the owner and context from the CSV cannot enter the decision message in v1.
  • The recipient is a static rule for this decision type; lumnik does not read the address from the entity.
  • lumnik compares a measurement with a declared threshold; it does not diagnose the cause. period=CLOSED comes from the source.
  • Grafana queries postgres every 5 seconds; this is not streaming.
  • Synthetic data.
