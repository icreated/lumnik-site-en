OPEN, then CLOSED, within a minute

A kind:Workflow declares a door's lifecycle over the enriched view: OPEN to start, CLOSED terminal, dormancy past one minute, and the sentence its decision says.

  1. 1
    Key and order

    door_incident_id follows each opening; occurred_at, the sensor's own time, picks its current event.

  2. 2
    Context

    The message cites the laboratory and the owner; fields adds the contents and the criticality.

What this step proves

The lifecycle is declared, not coded.

manifests/40-door-workflow.yaml
apiVersion: apps.lumnik.io/v1kind: Workflowmetadata:  name: scenario-freezer-door-fspec:  scope: cold-chain  provenance: declared  on:    table: connector.v_scenariofreezerdooreventf    column: door_state    key: door_incident_id    order: occurred_at  dormancy:    after: 1m    message: "{freezer_id} ({laboratory}): door {state} since {since}. Owner: {owner}"    fields: [contents, criticality]  states:    - code: OPEN      label: "Open"      initial: true    - code: CLOSED      label: "Closed"      terminal: true  transitions:    - { from: [OPEN], to: CLOSED }

Limits

  • The workflow knows the last event received: a lasting OPEN may also be a CLOSED lost before the broker. It detects silence, not the physical door.
  • Dormancy counts from lumnik's first observation, not from the sensor's occurred_at.
  • For the demonstration the sweep runs every 5 seconds; it runs every 15 minutes by default. The business threshold stays the minimum accepted, 1 minute.
  • Synthetic data.
