Limits

  • The workflow knows the last event received: a lasting OPEN may also be a CLOSED lost before the broker. It detects silence, not the physical door.
  • Dormancy counts from lumnik's first observation, not from the sensor's occurred_at.
  • For the demonstration the sweep runs every 5 seconds; it runs every 15 minutes by default. The business threshold stays the minimum accepted, 1 minute.
  • Synthetic data.
