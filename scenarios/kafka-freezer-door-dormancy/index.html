OPEN, then CLOSED, within a minute

The Workflow declares a door's lifecycle over the enriched view.

  • OPEN to start, CLOSED terminal
  • Dormancy past one minute
  • The sentence of the decision
  1. 1
    Key and order

    door_incident_id follows each opening. occurred_at, the sensor's own time, picks the current event.

  2. 2
    Context

    The message cites the laboratory and the owner. fields adds the contents and the criticality.

Limits

  • Workflow knows the last message received, not the door. A lasting OPEN may be a CLOSED lost before Kafka.
  • Dormancy counts from the message's arrival in lumnik, not from the sensor's occurred_at.
  • Demonstration: sweep every 5 seconds. Default: every 15 minutes. Business threshold: 1 minute, the minimum accepted.
  • Synthetic data.

The problem

The information exists, in two systems.

  • Kafka receives the door state: FZ-17, OPEN.
  • A CSV file gives the laboratory, the contents and the owner.
  • Neither one reports a door left open.

Nothing is changed

The sensor, Kafka and the file stay in place.

  • The Kafka producer does not change.
  • The CSV file stays where it is.
  • No application to build.

What had to be written

dormancy:
  after: 1m

This line sets the delay: 1 minute.

The whole scenario fits in 4 declaration files, 83 lines.

What the team receives

The decision names the laboratory and the owner.

Real message, replay of 26 October 2026.

Who sees what

The scope bounds what is read.

scope: cold-chain
  • Every file declares the scope cold-chain.
  • Keycloak grants the role scope:cold-chain.
  • Other users see neither this data nor these decisions.

The limit

lumnik reports a silence, not an open door.

  • The last state received is OPEN.
  • The CLOSED message may have been lost before Kafka.
  • The decision says what lumnik observed.
