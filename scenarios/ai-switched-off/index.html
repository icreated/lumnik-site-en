The AI switched off for an audit: switched off is not absent

Goal Switch the hub's AI off without changing edition or losing data, keep reading the sources, and know what to redo when it is switched back on.

A hospital pharmacy sends the hub its dispensing orders every day. The data protection officer asks for the AI to be switched off for the length of an audit.

  • the operator sets LUMNIK_AI_ENABLED=false and restarts the hub: rag and llm stay installed, said INACTIVE;
  • ask, search and chat answer the same sentence; the corpus is kept, and the hub says so;
  • the files are still read; a lifecycle declared during the audit is not indexed, and the apply says so.

Limits

  • The setting applies to the whole hub: the AI cannot be switched off for one scope only.
  • The hub must be restarted, once to switch off, once to switch back on.
  • During the audit nothing is indexed for chat: not a Workflow, not a connector described again, not a file loaded by lm csv ingest. They must be applied again.
  • lm rag reindex rebuilds only the platform corpus, not the scopes'.
  • A search by a user without a scope reads every lifecycle of the tenant, not only the pharmacy's.
  • Orders, wards and quantities synthetic.

Hospital pharmacy · audit

The DPO asks for the AI to be switched off.

For the length of an audit. Without changing edition, without losing data.

LUMNIK_AI_ENABLED=false

The problem

Switched off looked like absent.

  • rag status said chunks: 0, provider: none.
  • A lifecycle declared during the audit stayed out of chat, without a word.

What is not changed

The same image, the same data.

  • The jars stay, the rag schema stays migrated.
  • The day's files are read as before.

What had to be written

LUMNIK_AI_ENABLED=false

One setting, one restart.

  • Then true when the audit ends.
  • And the lifecycle applied a second time.

What was fixed

rag status says ai: switched off and what it keeps (#1895).

  • The workflow apply warns it does not index (#1896).
  • One sentence for ask, search and chat (#1894).
