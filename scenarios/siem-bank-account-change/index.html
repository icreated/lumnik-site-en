A changed bank account the SIEM never sees

Goal Let the SIEM see a supplier's IBAN change, without the IBAN showing in the inbox.

The ERP exports its suppliers as CSV. It emits no event. A bank-account change goes unnoticed until the next payment.

  • lumnik rereads the export every minute
  • a signed decision goes to the SIEM by webhook
  • the SIEM alerts on the account that changes
  • the inbox never shows the account.

The account goes to the SIEM, not into the sentence

Process reads each row written to the table.

  • event: data.row.changed: the key of the processing. The Process fires on each new or changed row; an unchanged row triggers nothing
  • when: status=ACTIVE: only active suppliers count
  • emit.message: the inbox sentence, without the account
  • emit.fields: [iban]: the account, in the signed payload only
  1. 1
    Why ACTIVE

    The calibration row is INACTIVE: it never reaches the SIEM. An inactive supplier is not paid: its account is no risk.

  2. 2
    Signed

    The webhook integration signs each delivery with its secret.

  3. 3
    Inbox

    The reader sees "Bank account observed for supplier SUP-0042 (Atelier Orion)", never the account.

Limits

  • Process sees a new ingested row. It does not compare it with the previous one: the SIEM correlates, as in production.
  • The full account crosses the signed webhook, for an exact fingerprint. In production: HTTPS, and restricted access to the delivery journal, which keeps payloads.
  • Synthetic SIEM. A real SIEM would apply its own rules.

The problem

  • A supplier's account is changed.
  • No alert leaves the ERP.
  • The SIEM receives nothing.
